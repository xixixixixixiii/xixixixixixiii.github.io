Capybara Survivor: GOGOGO!

Developer: yibu

Capybara Survivor: GOGOGO! is a casual strategy game combining tower defense, opposing forces, and roguelike upgrades. Build your team of companions, strengthen your equipment and talents, upgrade your carriage, and battle waves of enemies.

View on Google Play

Sign-in and Cloud Saves

On Android, the game uses Google Play Games sign-in with Firebase Authentication to identify your game account. Account identifiers are used to associate your account with its saved game progress.

Game progress is stored using Firebase so that players can save and retrieve progress associated with their game account.

Analytics and Error Reports

The game uses Google Analytics to understand how players use the game, including player retention, session duration, and in-game purchase events. This information helps us evaluate and improve the game.

We also collect error reports and related diagnostic information to identify technical problems and improve game stability.
